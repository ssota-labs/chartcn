## Range marks

A third price mark: the high–low span drawn as a translucent bar with a solid
tick at the close. It reads better than a candle when the open matters less
than the range itself.

The engine takes the mark type as `price`, so the same viewport, axes and
crosshair serve candles, a line, or these range bars without any of them
knowing about the others.

## Shared engine

All the Markets charts are configuration over one renderer. A chart declares
its price mark, any overlays that share the price scale, and any panes that
need their own — the viewport, axes, crosshair and interaction come from
`canvas-chart.tsx`.
