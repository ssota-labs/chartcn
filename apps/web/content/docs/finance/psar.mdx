## A points mark

SAR is a level per bar, not a curve, so joining the values with a line would
misrepresent it — hence a `points` mark. Dots below price and above it mean
opposite things, so they take the two-colour treatment as well.

The acceleration factor steps up each time the trend makes a new extreme,
which is why the dots visibly converge on price before a flip.
