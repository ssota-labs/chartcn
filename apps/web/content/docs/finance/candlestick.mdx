## Canvas renderer

Recharts has no candlestick series, and it is the wrong shape of tool here
anyway: one DOM node per mark, and no notion of a viewport you can move. An
exchange chart is a long series with a moving window, where panning is the
primary interaction rather than hover.

So the Markets charts share a renderer instead — `canvas-chart.tsx`. Grid,
axes, marks and crosshair readouts are all painted onto one canvas, only the
visible slice is drawn, and pan/zoom cause no React renders at all.

Drag to pan, wheel to zoom. The zoom is anchored on the bar under the cursor,
which is what makes it feel attached to the chart rather than to the viewport.

## Shared engine

All the Markets charts are configuration over one renderer. A chart declares
its price mark, any overlays that share the price scale, and any panes that
need their own — the viewport, axes, crosshair and interaction come from
`canvas-chart.tsx`.
