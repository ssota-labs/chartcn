## A channel from the data itself

No smoothing and no standard deviation: the upper edge is simply the highest
high of the last 20 bars and the lower edge the lowest low. Price touching an
edge means it has made a new extreme for the window, which is the entire signal.

Rendered as a `band` overlay, the same mark Bollinger uses — the difference is
entirely in how the two edges are computed.

## Shared engine

Like the rest of the Markets charts this is configuration over
`canvas-chart.tsx` — the indicator is a pure function over the series, and the
chart declares where its output belongs: an overlay on the price scale, or a
pane with its own.
