## Slope, not level

Every bar adds its volume to a running total, positive if the close rose and
negative if it fell. The absolute level is arbitrary — it depends entirely on
where the series happens to start — so OBV is read by its slope and by
divergence from price.

That is why the pane derives its extent from what is visible rather than
anchoring at zero: the useful question is which way it is going here, not how
far it is from an arbitrary origin.

## Shared engine

Like the rest of the Markets charts this is configuration over
`canvas-chart.tsx` — the indicator is a pure function over the series, and the
chart declares where its output belongs: an overlay on the price scale, or a
pane with its own.
