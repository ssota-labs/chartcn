## Session anchoring

VWAP is the average price *so far today*, weighted by volume. A running total
over the whole series would drift into meaninglessness after a few days, so the
accumulator resets when the UTC date rolls over — the line restarts each
session, which is the shape traders expect.

It sits on the price scale as a `line` overlay, so the extent scan includes it.

## Shared engine

Like the rest of the Markets charts this is configuration over
`canvas-chart.tsx` — the indicator is a pure function over the series, and the
chart declares where its output belongs: an overlay on the price scale, or a
pane with its own.
