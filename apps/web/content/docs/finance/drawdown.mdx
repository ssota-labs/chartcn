## Line price with an area pane

The price pane here is a line rather than candles — for an equity curve the
open and the wicks are noise. The drawdown is an `area` item in its own pane,
filled down to a baseline at zero with a guide on the same level.

Drawdown is always ≤ 0 by construction: it is the percentage below the running
peak, so the curve touches zero exactly at each new high.

## Shared engine

All the Markets charts are configuration over one renderer. A chart declares
its price mark, any overlays that share the price scale, and any panes that
need their own — the viewport, axes, crosshair and interaction come from
`canvas-chart.tsx`.
