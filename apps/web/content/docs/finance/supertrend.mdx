## One line, two colours

The line is drawn as runs of constant direction, each stroked in its own
colour. The run also *breaks* at a flip rather than joining through it: at a
reversal the value jumps from one ATR band to the other, and connecting those
two points would draw a segment that never existed.

The bands ratchet — they only ever tighten toward price while a trend holds,
and reset when it flips. That is what keeps the stop from loosening on a pullback.

## Shared engine

Like the rest of the Markets charts this is configuration over
`canvas-chart.tsx`. This one needed the renderer to grow a capability first —
the marks the other charts use were not enough to express it honestly.
