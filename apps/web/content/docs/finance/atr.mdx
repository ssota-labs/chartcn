## Range, not direction

True range is the bar's own span widened by any gap from the previous close, so
a market that opens away from yesterday is counted as volatile even if its own
candle is small. ATR then smooths that the Wilder way, matching RSI rather than
a plain mean.

ATR is in price units and unbounded, so its pane derives its extent from the
visible slice instead of pinning a domain.
