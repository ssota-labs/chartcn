## A pane of its own

Volume cannot share the price axis — the numbers have nothing to do with each
other. It gets a pane: its own band of the plot with its own extent, recomputed
from whatever is visible.

Panes are the engine's unit for exactly this. Each declares a `share` of the
plot height and either derives its scale from its items or pins it with
`domain`. Bars are tinted by the direction of their candle.

## Shared engine

All the Markets charts are configuration over one renderer. A chart declares
its price mark, any overlays that share the price scale, and any panes that
need their own — the viewport, axes, crosshair and interaction come from
`canvas-chart.tsx`.
