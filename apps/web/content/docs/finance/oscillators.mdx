## Two scales that cannot be shared

RSI is bounded 0–100 and MACD is centred on zero. Neither can sit on the price
axis, so each takes a pane.

RSI pins its scale with `domain: [0, 100]` — deriving it from the visible
values would make the 30/70 guides drift, and the whole point of those levels
is that they are fixed. MACD derives its own extent but adds a guide at zero,
so the histogram reads as above or below the signal.

MACD uses Wilder-style EMAs for the 12/26 pair and a 9-period EMA for the
signal; RSI uses Wilder's smoothing rather than a plain average, which is what
makes it RSI.

## Shared engine

All the Markets charts are configuration over one renderer. A chart declares
its price mark, any overlays that share the price scale, and any panes that
need their own — the viewport, axes, crosshair and interaction come from
`canvas-chart.tsx`.
