## Two-tone fill

Span A and span B cross, and which one is on top is the whole signal — so the
band mark grew a second colour. The fill is split into runs of constant sign
and each run drawn in its own colour.

The split happens at the exact crossing, solved between the two bars, not at
the next bar boundary. Snapping to a bar would leave the two fills overlapping
by one bar, which shows as a notch at every cross.

Both spans are plotted 26 bars ahead of the data they summarise, which is why
the cloud leads price.
