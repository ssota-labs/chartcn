## Where the close sits in its range

%K asks where the close falls between the window's high and low, as a
percentage. %D is a short moving average of %K, so the pair crossing is the
conventional read.

Like RSI, this pins `domain: [0, 100]`. Deriving the scale from visible values
would let the 20/80 bands drift, and being fixed is what makes those levels
mean anything.

## Shared engine

Like the rest of the Markets charts this is configuration over
`canvas-chart.tsx` — the indicator is a pure function over the series, and the
chart declares where its output belongs: an overlay on the price scale, or a
pane with its own.
