## Band overlay

This is the clearest case for widening the price extent with overlay values.
The bands routinely sit outside the visible high and low — that is the whole
point of them — so a scale derived from the candles alone would clip the
envelope at the pane edge.

A `band` overlay takes an upper and a lower series. The engine fills between
them at low alpha and strokes both edges faintly, so the envelope reads as one
shape rather than two unrelated lines.

## Shared engine

its price mark, any overlays that share the price scale, and any panes that
